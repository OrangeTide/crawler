# Modular kits, one per tileset. A kit names the baked mesh pieces that
# clad that tileset's sectors, each with its footprint in grid units, so
# the theme G cladding pass places them at native scale over the logical
# grid. A tileset with no kit here renders with the procedural surface
# quads as before.
#
# kit <tileset> <name>
#   <role> <LUMP> <fw> <fh> [<fz>]
# roles: floor ceil wall pillar; fw fh are the ground footprint in grid
# units, fz the height in units (default 1).

# Tileset 9: the SigilsVault mossy dungeon kit. Pieces authored at native
# ~4 metre scale, so the floor and ceiling cover a 4x4 block and the wall
# spans 4 units and stands 3 tall. See content/rooms/pieces.
kit 9 mossy
  floor  MOD_FLOOR   4 4
  ceil   MOD_CEIL    4 4
  wall   MOD_WALL    4 1 3
  pillar MOD_PILLAR  1 1 3
