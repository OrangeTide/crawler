NPC portrait art
================

The portrait PNGs in this directory are from "500 Fantasy Pixel Art
Portraits" by Batareya, obtained from batareya.itch.io:

  https://batareya.itch.io/500-fantasy-pixel-art-portraits-a-world-of-characters-for-your-projects

Each file is one portrait from that pack, scaled to the game's 64x64
format. The pack is numbered. The NPC portraits used here are, by dialog id:

  port_elder       136
  port_barkeep     459
  port_apothecary  457
  port_ranger      454
  port_hunter      217
  port_dockhand    208

The player-character portrait pool, pc00 through pc23, maps to pack entries:

  pc00 1    pc06 85   pc12 245  pc18 434
  pc01 9    pc07 108  pc13 278  pc19 480
  pc02 23   pc08 119  pc14 293  pc20 500
  pc03 31   pc09 141  pc15 306  pc21 213
  pc04 45   pc10 160  pc16 356  pc22 358
  pc05 74   pc11 212  pc17 420  pc23 362
