NPC portrait art
================

The portrait PNGs in this directory are third-party art by a third-party artist,
obtained from a third-party source. They are bundled here for use within
this game, not for standalone redistribution.

port_elder, port_barkeep, port_apothecary, port_ranger, port_hunter come
from the "a portrait pack". port_dockhand comes from the "Old
Villager Portrait" pack.

  and commercial projects.
- Feel free to modify the assets to suit your unique needs. While credit is
  not required, we genuinely appreciate any attribution given.
- You may not redistribute or resell the assets on their own, that includes
  NFT/images asset compilations.

Created using a local generative tool for rough concepts, then refined by
hand for consistency and quality.
